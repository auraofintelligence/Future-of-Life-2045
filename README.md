# Future of Life 2045

A visual, interactive public site connecting Luke Nathan Hayes's long-running future plan with projects being built now across relationships, living systems, intelligence, movement, science and culture.

Luke has developed the larger plan since 2010. In 2022, he adapted part of it for the Future of Life Institute Worldbuilding Contest. The entry did not become a finalist, but the experiment produced two day-in-the-life stories that later informed a detailed 2022 to 2045 workbook timeline.

## The site

- explores two source-grounded days in 2045
- presents global group marriage as a positive family framework at household, U.N. of Love and 400-plus-adult scales
- includes an uncapped family constellation model, a dedicated source-led Subterranean Cities of Light destination, horn-torus geometry and the 2045 timeline
- separates operating underground precedents, proposed integration pathways and the authored 2045 civilisation
- connects the wider public project ecosystem
- labels original vision, active public work and proposed or speculative horizons separately
- uses generated raster imagery and code-based interaction, with no SVG assets

Open `index.html` directly or serve the folder with any small static web server.

## Source boundaries

The three original 2045 files remain private source documents. This public site paraphrases and quotes selected material without publishing the files themselves. Scenario events are not presented as verified history.
