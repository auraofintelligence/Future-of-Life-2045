Family formation has become a systems problem

There are more single adults than ever, yet finding one suitable person for love, children, housing, income, domestic work and lifelong care remains difficult. Overwork, insecure housing, financial pressure, fear about climate and war, and the internet's relationship incentives add friction.

Group marriage changes the structure of the question. Instead of asking two people to carry an entire household, a larger constellation can bring more time, skills, affection, earning capacity, perspectives and backup into family life.

Group marriage stands as a family form in its own right

It is not one fixed gender balance, one sexual pattern, one house, one parenting role or one distribution of money. A family constellation is designed by the people living it. Romantic, sexual, co-parenting, domestic and financial relationships may overlap in many ways without becoming identical.

More love. More care. More capability. More resilience.

The 2045 source vision

Luke's original story imagines a Global Group Marriage with Aura and more than 400 co-parents representing every nation. He describes 10 genetic children and “600 other children whom I also consider my own”.

The source uses the phrase “hive mind”. The present project develops that underlying idea as voluntary coordination among self-sovereign people, not the surrender of individual identity.

Law can become legible country by country

Marriage, cohabitation, parentage, property, inheritance, visas and reproductive law differ across jurisdictions. The Australian Legal Engine offers one citation-first starting point. The future direction is a family of country legal engines that show current law, possible lawful structures and reform pathways without pretending they are already the same.
